A farm records app I designed and built for my mom, to replace the notebooks she used to track her farm. FAMA puts the farm’s money and milk in one place, with big type, clear icons and plain words, and it works even without internet.

01 / Overview

Built for
my mom.

My mom runs cows, chickens, fish and avocados, and her records lived in notebooks that were hard to add up. The brief was personal: an app she would actually open every day on her phone, often with no signal, without needing anyone to explain it.

Product designDesigned with a real userOffline-first developmentMilk & money trackingMonthly reports & Excel exportInstallable mobile app
02 / Approach

Big, clear,
and simple.

Every decision started from one question: can Mom use this without help? That meant large fonts and big numbers, clear icons for each part of the farm, generous tap targets and one obvious action per screen. Labels use everyday words — “Which side is earning?”, “People you work with”, “Add your vet first” — instead of accounting terms. Records save on the phone first and sync online when there is signal, so nothing is lost in the field.

04 / Result

From notebooks to one tap.

FAMA is live and in use on Mom’s farm, showing earnings, expenses and profit for each part of the farm, with monthly reports she can export straight to Excel. Simple enough for her, useful for any smallholder farmer.
