Website Design & Development

Belgravia
Mall.

A modern commercial-property website created to present Belgravia Mall’s retail spaces, architectural character and leasing opportunities through a polished, image-led digital experience.
RoleWebsite Designer & Developer
TypeCommercial property & retail
LocationNairobi, Kenya
01 / Overview

A refined digital presence.

Belgravia Mall required a refined digital presence that could introduce the development, communicate its commercial value and help prospective tenants explore available opportunities. I designed and developed a responsive, image-led website that presents the mall’s architecture, location advantages, retail offering and leasing information in a clear and visually engaging format.

Website designResponsive interface developmentContent structure & visual hierarchyImage-led property presentationLeasing-focused calls to actionMobile & tablet optimisation
02 / Challenge

Premium visuals.
Practical information.

The project needed to balance a premium visual identity with practical commercial information. Large architectural images had to remain impactful without overwhelming the content, while prospective tenants needed a straightforward way to understand the mall’s location, features and leasing potential.
03 / Solution

Image-led and
easy to explore.

I created a clean, responsive website built around strong architectural imagery, clear content hierarchy and focused calls to action. The design uses generous spacing, structured content sections and Belgravia’s turquoise and magenta colour palette to create a consistent experience across desktop, tablet and mobile.
