Logo Design & Brand Identity

GR
Continental.

A logo and brand identity for GR Continental Care Ltd, a medical skin care company. The mark pairs a confident wordmark with a sweeping global arc, then carries it across stationery and vehicle branding.
RoleBrand & Logo Designer
TypeMedical skin care brand
LocationNairobi, Kenya
01 / Overview

Care with a
global reach.

GR Continental needed an identity that felt clinical and trustworthy while signalling ambition beyond a single market. The logo had to work at every size, from a business card to the side of a service van.

Logo designBrand colour systemBusiness cardsLetterheadVehicle brandingBrand applications
02 / Approach

One arc.
Many touchpoints.

The “O” in Continental becomes an infinity loop that lifts into a globe-like arc, a nod to continuous care and continental reach. A sky-blue to mint gradient keeps the palette fresh and medical, grounded by a strong charcoal wordmark for legibility.
