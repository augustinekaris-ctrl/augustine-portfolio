Event Badge & Print Design

Event
Lanyards.

Lanyard badges for two regional conferences where Riana Group was technology partner: the Africa CX Leaders Forum 2026 in Kigali, and the 21st Annual National Co-operatives Leaders Conference in Mombasa for Co-op Consultancy, part of the Co-operative Bank Group.
RoleDesign Team Lead
TypeEvent badges & print
EventsKigali · Mombasa, 2026
01 / Overview

Small format,
big first impression.

A badge is the first branded thing every delegate touches and wears all day. Each one had to carry the host’s identity, the event details and the technology partner credit, while leaving the delegate’s name as the hero.

Badge layoutFront & back designDelegate name systemEvent information hierarchyPartner brandingPrint-ready artwork
02 / Approach

Built from
each host’s brand.

For the CX Leaders Forum, a photographic blue header holds the event title, theme, dates and venue, with a clean white panel below for the delegate’s name and organisation, and a patterned back that still reads from across the room. For the Co-operatives Conference, I created two directions in Co-op green and gold with a geometric border drawn from the brand: one bold with a full-width green band, one lighter with a vertical pattern strip. Every badge credits Riana Group as technology partner in the same footer position.
