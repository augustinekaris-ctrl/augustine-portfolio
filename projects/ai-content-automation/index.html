01 / The problem

Publishing takes more than writing.

Creating a useful blog post involves research, writing, formatting, imagery, review and publishing. I built the workflow to connect those steps while preserving a deliberate approval point before anything goes live.

03 / Blogging agent

From Telegram to WordPress.

Content awareness

Checks published content and builds a URL inventory before producing the article.

AI writing

Generates the complete article and passes it through a validation and normalisation step.

Featured imagery

Generates the article image, converts it for the workflow and prepares it for review.

Human approval

Sends the image to Telegram and routes approved and rejected outcomes separately.

WordPress publishing

Uploads the approved image, sets alt text and publishes the article with the featured image.

Confirmation

Returns a Telegram confirmation after successful publishing.
