Brand Identity & Website Design

OG
Makowade.

A brand identity and website for OG Makowade Advocates, a Nairobi law firm. A quill-topped “g” monogram and a classic serif wordmark give the firm a look of tradition and authority, carried through to its website, stationery and office signage.
RoleBrand & Web Designer
TypeLaw firm
LocationNairobi, Kenya
01 / Overview

Authority,
with a signature.

OG Makowade needed an identity that clients would trust on first sight, then a website that carried the same confidence online. Everything had to feel established, considered and unmistakably legal.

Logo & monogramBrand colour systemWebsite designBusiness cardsOffice signageBranded stationery
02 / Approach

Ink, quill
and gold.

The monogram draws the “g” as a stroke of ink resting on a base line, topped with a quill — a quiet reference to written law and signed agreements. Deep burgundy and gold set a premium, traditional tone, and the website pairs that palette with courtroom and library imagery so the firm feels the same online as it does in the office.
