01 / Overview

Technical topics,
made scrollable.

Riana sells building management systems, AI surveillance, entrance control and CX technology to large organisations. The challenge was turning dense technical offerings into posts that stop the scroll and still read as credible to decision-makers.

LinkedIn post designSwipeable carouselsProduct educationEvent speaker graphicsSeasonal campaignsBrand consistency
02 / Approach

One look,
many stories.

Every post works from the same toolkit: Riana’s teal gradient, white logo lockup, bold Poppins headlines with an orange accent word and clean iconography. Product posts lead with a single question (“How Smart Is Your ‘Smart’ Building?”), carousels break one topic into numbered, swipeable steps — like the five pedestrian entrance systems — and event and milestone posts reuse the same frame so the feed reads as one brand.
