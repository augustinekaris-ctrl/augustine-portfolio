Granola,
made to shop.

Open Secret needed a storefront that felt as fresh as its packaging: something that showed off each blend, made the range easy to compare and kept the path to cart short on any device.

Website designFront-end developmentProduct carouselCollection & pricingBrand storytellingMobile optimisation
02 / Approach

Let the pack
lead the page.

Each blend gets its own colour moment, so the hero shifts with the product: teal for Fusion, purple for Chocolate Dreams, orange for Tropical. Clean Plus Jakarta Sans type, generous white space and a dark “Breakfast Ritual” section give the brand a premium rhythm, while prices in KSh and one-tap Add to Cart keep it practical for local shoppers.
