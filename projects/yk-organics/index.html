Brand Identity & Packaging Design

YK
Organics.

A logo, packaging and brand identity for YK Organics, a Kenyan organic skincare brand. A hand-lettered “yk” grows out of a leafy stem, finished in rose gold against matte black for a look that feels natural and premium at the same time.
RoleBrand & Packaging Designer
TypeOrganic skincare brand
LocationNairobi, Kenya
01 / Overview

Natural,
made premium.

YK Organics needed an identity that signalled clean, plant-based ingredients without looking homemade, and that could sit on a shelf next to established beauty brands.

Logo designBrand colour systemProduct packagingJar & label designShop signageBusiness cards
02 / Approach

Rooted in
the leaf.

The monogram is drawn as a single flowing line: a stem with leaves that becomes the “y” and “k”, then curves into a base for the word Organics. Rose gold on matte black keeps the packaging bold and giftable, while the soft blush version carries the brand onto lighter stationery.

A shelf-ready skincare brand.
